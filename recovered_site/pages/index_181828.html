Flight makes the imagination Limitless...


Spacecraft Replicas is dedicated to the creation of  models of real spacecraft and spaceships. While this is often called modeling, we want to address all scales of modeling up to full scale. This website is initially intended to serve as a central resource for making unique replica or models of the Mercury Spacecraft, eventually leading to the construction of a full scale replica. This is NOT intended to be a commercial venture. Any plans, ideas, drawings, construction methods, and information contained herein are free for personal use or any other non-commercial ventures. Anyone wishing to use this information for commercial purposes should obtain written permission.
