Here are a few of the 25 unique molds for the exterior surface of the Mercury Capsule. Also pictured is a prototype part of the lift ring cover. This one was molded in white and painted. It was test fitted on an actual capsule. Beside the color being slightly off, the size and dimples were nearly exact replicas. These molds are much more accurate than other replicas often seen. This is an early computer model of the Antenna Fairing top and some of the lower shingles. Colin does excellent work!
